Safety-first model

Your assistant should not blindly change a project.

fls-pilot is designed around a controlled workflow: scan first, explain findings, propose one reversible action, ask for approval, then report before and after.

1Scan first

Read available FL Studio context without changing the project.

2Explain findings

Translate technical risks into plain producer language.

3Propose one action

Keep the next step small, scoped and reversible.

4Ask for approval

No persistent edit happens without explicit confirmation.

5Report & rollback

Show what changed, what was verified and how to roll back.

FAQ

Clear expectations sell better than hype.

Does fls-pilot make music for me?

No. It is designed to help you review technical and workflow details while you stay in creative control.

Does it work with local AI?

Yes, where your chosen local assistant or MCP-capable client supports the workflow. fls-pilot can provide structured FL Studio context and test results for interactive review.

Will it change my FL Studio project automatically?

No persistent project edit should be presented as automatic. The safety model is read-only first, then explanation, proposal, approval and rollback-capable reporting.

Can the test structure grow?

Yes. The workflow structure is designed to grow with deeper checks, additional review modules and future genre-aware packs. That does not mean arbitrary automatic changes; the safety model still stays approval-first.

What does Founder Edition include?

Founder Edition includes v3.0 access, setup guidance, reasonable email support for setup and usage questions, and early access to stable fixes and improvements in the v3.0 branch. Separate Pro packs, genre packs and deeper workflow expansions may still be sold as paid add-ons later.

Is FL Studio included?

No. FL Studio is required separately. fls-pilot is an independent local workflow assistant for FL Studio and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Image-Line.
